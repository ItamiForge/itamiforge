---
title: YieldViz
description: Analytics concept to track yield leakage and translate waste to cost.
---

## Problem

Breweries often discover yield loss too late, with poor visibility into where the loss actually happened.

## Product concept

YieldViz maps volume transitions across brewing stages and surfaces the highest financial leak points.

## Core ideas

- Granular stage-by-stage tracking.
- Visual flow communication (Sankey-driven analysis).
- Translate technical loss to dollar loss for decision-makers.
